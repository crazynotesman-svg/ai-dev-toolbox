introduction: |
  Base64 encoding is one of the most widely used techniques for representing binary data as plain ASCII text. It powers email attachments, data URLs, JSON web tokens, and countless API payloads where text-based transports are required. While every language ships a built-in encoder, developers still reach for an online tool constantly: to quickly encode a string before sending it to a colleague, to decode a token they received from a service, or to verify that a piece of Base64 really decodes to what they expect.

  The Base64 Encoder/Decoder at AI Developer Toolbox converts plain text to Base64 and back with full UTF-8 support, so Chinese characters, emoji, and other multi-byte text survive the round trip without corruption. Everything runs locally in your browser: your input never leaves your device, there is nothing to install, and there is no registration. The tool also validates incoming Base64 and gives you a clear error message instead of silently producing garbage when the input is malformed.

  This page walks through how to encode text, how to decode Base64, what the tool checks for you, and answers the most common questions about Base64 encoding and its limits.

faqs:
  - question: Is the Base64 encoder/decoder free to use?
    answer: Yes, the tool is completely free, requires no registration, and has no usage limits. It works in any modern browser.
  - question: Does it support Chinese characters and emoji?
    answer: Yes. The tool is based on UTF-8 byte encoding, so Chinese, Japanese, emoji, and other multi-byte characters are encoded and decoded losslessly with consistent round trips.
  - question: Is Base64 encryption?
    answer: No. Base64 is an encoding scheme, not encryption. It is fully reversible and offers no security, so never use it to protect sensitive data. Use a proper encryption algorithm instead.

    answer: The decoder validates the character set, padding, and length rules and reports a specific error message, so you know exactly what is wrong instead of receiving corrupted output.
